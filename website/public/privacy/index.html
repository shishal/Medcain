Privacy policy

This policy describes how MEDCAIN (the Android app com.shishal.medico and this website at medico.shishal.com) handles personal data. MEDCAIN is operated by Shishal (“we”, “us”).

MEDCAIN is an education app for Indian MBBS university exams. It is not a clinical product and is intended for people 18 and older.

Who this applies to

People who create a MEDCAIN account, use the app, or visit this website. If you do not agree with this policy, do not create an account.

Data we collect

We collect only what the product needs. We do not show ads. We do not sell your data.

  • Email — required to sign in (Supabase Auth). Used for account, support, and matching a payment to your plan.
  • Name and phone — optional profile fields. Used in the app as a faint watermark on test content so leaked screenshots can be traced. You can leave them blank; the watermark then uses email.
  • App activity — PYQs you open, practice sessions, answers, scores where applicable, bookmarks, learnt lessons, and practice filters. Required for the product to work.
  • Plan status — Free, Pro, or Elite, and when a paid window expires. Set by our servers after a verified payment, not by the app sending us a “I paid” flag.
  • Screenshot events — if the app cannot block a screenshot (typical on iOS), we may log that an event happened against your account. We do not receive the screenshot image.
  • Payment details — card and UPI data are handled by Razorpay. We receive payment identifiers, amount, and status so we can grant the plan. We do not store full card numbers.
  • This website — standard request logs on the machine that hosts the site (IP, user agent, path). We do not run advertising cookies or a marketing pixel.

We do not collect location, contacts, photos from your library, SMS, or financial information inside the Android app. The app’s sensitive permission is internet access only.

Why we collect it

  • Provide PYQs, practice scoring, bookmarks, and plan-gated content.
  • Keep your session signed in and restore in-progress practice.
  • Apply a paid plan after a successful website checkout.
  • Respond to support, refund, and deletion requests.
  • Discourage sharing of paid question content (watermark / screenshot log).

We do not use this data for advertising or “personalization” in the Play Store advertising sense. We do not share it with data brokers.

Where it is stored and who sees it

Account and question-bank data live in our Supabase project (hosted Postgres + Auth). Traffic to that backend is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

Processors we use to run the product:

  • Supabase — authentication and database.
  • Google Play / Apple (when iOS ships) — app distribution.
  • Razorpay — payments, only when you pay on this website.
  • Cloudflare — DNS and reverse proxy in front of this static site.

We do not share your email, name, phone, or test activity with other third parties for their marketing. We may disclose data if required by law.

How long we keep it

Account and attempt history stay for as long as the account exists, so you can reopen results and bookmarks. If you ask us to delete the account, we delete or anonymize personal data we control, except records we must keep for tax, fraud, or legal reasons (for example a payment receipt). Website server logs are rotated in the ordinary course of running the host.

Your choices

  • Access / correction — you can edit name and phone in the app profile. Email is your login; write to us to change it.
  • Account deletion — email support@medico.shishal.com from the address on the account. We will confirm and delete. Details: Account, cancel & refunds.
  • Paid plan — access ends when the prepaid window expires. See the account page for cancel / refund.

Children

MEDCAIN is not directed at children. Do not create an account if you are under 18.

This website and checkout

The marketing and legal pages are static files. Checkout at /checkout uses the same MEDCAIN email login. That page loads our public backend keys only (not payment secrets).

Changes

If this policy changes in a material way, we will update the date at the top of this page. Continued use after that date means you accept the update.
