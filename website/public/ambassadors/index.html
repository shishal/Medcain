Become a MEDCAIN ambassador

What you do

  • Be the point of contact for your college batch.
  • Help classmates start the 4-day Pro trial.
  • Share internal / university dates and papers we can add.

What you get

  • Pro access for your ambassador term.
  • Direct line to the product team.

There is no in-app CRM yet — apply by emailing medcain@shishal.com with your college, MBBS year, and university register number.
