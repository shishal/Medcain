How it works

Home → subject → exam year → answer

Real university papers mix chapters in one sitting. MEDCAIN mirrors that: years and papers first, chapters as filters.

Pick your university

State, affiliating university, college, and MBBS year at onboarding — editable later on Profile.

Open a subject

Home shows coverage for your year. Open a subject to see its exam years.

Read the paper

Long answers and short notes in tabs. Filter by chapter or Paper I / II when you need to narrow in.

DA, then EX

Direct answer opens first. Explanation adds textbook pages and topic links when available.

What you get

University PYQ bank

Theory stems tagged by exam year, paper, marks, and how often they repeat — for the affiliating university you pick in the app.

Direct answer & explanation

Book, edition, and page on the question. We cite standard texts; we do not host PDFs. Open your own copy to the page.

FAQ

Before you install

Is this a NEET-PG mock-test app?

No. MEDCAIN is for MBBS university exams — previous-year theory questions with answers and textbook page refs. Timed mock catalogs are not the home experience.

Is this a medical or clinical tool?

No. It is exam prep only. Not a medical device and not clinical advice.

Which university is supported?

You pick your affiliating university in the app. PYQ coverage depends on what has been tagged for that university so far.

Do you take payment inside the Android app?

No. Google Play Billing is not used. Plans are purchased on this website. The app only opens this site in your browser.

Who is it for?

Adults in MBBS (18+) at Indian affiliating universities. It is not directed at children.

Where is the app available?

Android first, via Google Play. Privacy and account pages live here for the store listing. iOS comes later.
