Manage plan, cancel, refunds & account deletion

Google Play and App Store listings link here so you can manage a plan without hunting through the app. Payments happen only on this website — not through Google Play Billing.

See your current plan

Open the MEDCAIN app → Profile. That screen shows Free, Pro, or Elite. New accounts start with a short Pro trial (currently 4 days). After you pay on the web, tap the refresh control on Profile; you do not need a new app install.

How billing works

  • Free is always available after the trial ends.
  • Pro is ₹1,499 for 180 days. Elite is ₹2,999 for 365 days.
  • These are prepaid access windows. They do not auto-renew. We will not charge you again unless you start a new checkout.
  • There is nothing to “unsubscribe” in Google Play, because Play never billed you.

Cancel

Because plans do not renew, cancel means: do not buy again when the window ends. Your Pro or Elite access continues until the end date shown in the app Profile; then the account returns to Free on its own.

If you want the paid plan removed before the end date (for example after a refund), email support@medico.shishal.com from the same address you use in the app. We will set the account back to Free.

Refunds

Digital question-bank access is generally non-refundable once you have used paid content. We will still consider a refund if:

  • You were charged in error (duplicate payment).
  • You request it within 7 days of payment and have not opened paid PYQ direct answers / explanations or started a practice session that required the paid plan.
  • Indian consumer law requires it in your situation.

Email support@medico.shishal.com with the email on the account and the Razorpay payment id if you have it. Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method via Razorpay. A Razorpay refund does not always change the plan by itself — write to us so we can set the account to Free.

To buy or renew, open checkout and sign in with the same email as the app.

Delete your account

Play’s user-data policy requires a way to request deletion. Email support@medico.shishal.com from the address on the MEDCAIN account. In the body, write “Please delete my MEDCAIN account.”

We will:

  • Confirm the request (usually within 7 days).
  • Delete the login and profile we control, including practice attempts and bookmarks tied to that user.
  • Keep only what we must for tax, dispute, or legal records (for example a payment receipt without your question-level history).

Uninstalling the app does not delete the account. You must email us.
